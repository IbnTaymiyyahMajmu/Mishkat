# Who asks whom: every question in the Qur'an, by the word that asks it.
#
#   surah:ayah:word   asker>asked   [@day | @death]   [?]
#
# One line to a question, in muṣḥaf order. The word is counted as the Quranic
# Arabic Corpus counts the words of an ayah. A question the corpus tags as
# interrogative must be here (scripts/build-questions.mjs refuses to build
# without it); a question it does not tag - a rhetorical am, an interrogative
# man it reads as a relative - is here because it was read and added by hand.
#
#   asker>asked   who puts the question, and to whom - or, where the question
#                 is about a party rather than to it, the party it confronts.
#                 The names are the ids in src/lib/quran/questionWho.ts.
#   indirect      a question inside a sentence: 'to see which of you is best
#                 in deed'. Counted, and shown apart.
#   x             not a question, though the corpus tags it as one.
#   @day          asked on the Day of Resurrection or after it.
#   @death        asked at death.
#   ?             the commentators differ on who is speaking; listed in
#                 docs/QUESTIONS-REVIEW.md for a reader of knowledge to settle.
#
# Written for this site from the text, the context of each ayah and the
# tafsir; not yet read by a scholar. See docs/DATA-NEEDED.md.
