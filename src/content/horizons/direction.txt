# The direction of the films — what the lexicon cannot be left to decide.
#
# `scripts/build-horizons.mjs` reads every word of the Qur'an from the
# corpus's morphology and finds the signs by their lemmas: شَمْس is the sun,
# جِبَال mountains, and مَآء sent down مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ is rain. It finds the
# unseen the same way — جَنَّة, جَهَنَّم, ٱلْقِيَٰمَة, مَلٰٓئِكَة — and from the
# word that names it to the end of its ayah the world is veiled. What follows
# is what a list of words gets wrong, set right by hand, one line to a point.
#
#   veil  81:1-14            the unseen, though no word names it outright:
#                            the sun wrapped, the mountains moving, the Garden
#                            described with no word for the Garden in it
#   see   13:4               this world, though a word for the unseen is in it:
#                            an earthly garden, a fire kindled, a throne of a
#                            king. A word for the unseen in a seen ayah is
#                            left alone: nothing is shown for it, nothing
#                            marks it, and the ayah's creation goes on.
#   quiet 69:7 palms         a sign the lexicon found that is not a sign here —
#                            most often a simile: "as if they were the trunks
#                            of hollow palms" is said of the dead. With no
#                            sign named, every sign in the ayah is dropped.
#   sign  24:40 deep at بحر  a sign set by hand, at the first word of the ayah
#                            carrying that root (or at its first word).
#
# The rules behind it are in src/lib/horizons/signs.ts and the reasons for
# the harder calls in docs/HORIZONS-REVIEW.md, by section letter.
