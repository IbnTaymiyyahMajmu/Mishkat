# The questions of the Qur'an — who is speaking, where the commentators differ

The questions page (`/questions/`) says who asks each question and of whom.
That was written for this site from the ayah, its context and the tafsir, and
it has not yet been read by a scholar. For most of the 1,033 questions there
is no real disagreement. For the sixteen below the commentators differ on the
speaker or on who is addressed, and the page shows a small sign beside each
question to say so.

**What is asked of you:** for each, keep the reading the page gives, or name
the other. The line to change is in `src/content/questions/who.txt` (the
number after the colon is the word that asks the question); then
`npm run gen:questions`.

The views listed are as I understood them from the tafsir held on the site
(al-Ṭabarī, al-Baghawī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Qurṭubī). Check each against them
before relying on it — that is the point of this sheet.
